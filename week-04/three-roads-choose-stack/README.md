# Three Roads: Choose Your Stack with AI

**Track:** General AI Fluency
**Estimated workload:** 2h
**Phase:** Build

## Brief

Full brief: [brief.md](brief.md)

Give AI the four real constraints, get three genuine stack options,
pressure-test the front-runner, decide with an honest rationale.

## What was delivered

- Four constraints stated against the actual sitemap/content map, not
  hypothetical ones.
- Three real options (plain HTML/CSS · React+Vite · Next.js+Vercel),
  simplest to most powerful, each with build/host/backend/trade-off.
- Pressure-tested the front-runner (React + Vite, already shipped in
  [Empty but Live](../empty-but-live/)) honestly: conceded that plain
  HTML is the more textbook-correct choice for what the site does
  *today* — the case for React+Vite is workflow-fit (it's the same
  stack behind Elite/DefensaOS/InmoDesk) and headroom, not a hard
  requirement the simpler option can't meet.
- Full document: [stack-choice.md](stack-choice.md).
