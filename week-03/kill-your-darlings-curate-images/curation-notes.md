# Curation notes — hero texture

Three options generated from the same iterated prompt (see
[image-list.md](image-list.md)), all holding the same style: dark
pine-green grid, faint blueprint annotations, sparse burnt-orange
accent marks.

## Kept: [hero-v2-keeper.png](hero-v2-keeper.png)

All four corners carry light annotation (circles, tick marks, dashed
guides), and the center stays completely clear. Reads as a deliberate
frame for whatever headline sits on top of it, not as an accident.

## Rejected: [hero-v1-rejected-unbalanced.png](hero-v1-rejected-unbalanced.png)

Annotations only cluster in two diagonal corners (top-left,
bottom-right) — the other two are bare. On a hero this reads as
lopsided rather than intentional, especially with the headline sitting
left-aligned per the sitemap.

## Rejected: [hero-v3-rejected-busy.png](hero-v3-rejected-busy.png)

The most visually interesting of the three in isolation, but it breaks
the identity kit's own rule — "an accent calm enough that the work is
the loudest thing on the page." It has thicker orange lines and an
arrow sitting in the upper-center area, right where a headline would
land, plus more active elements overall. As a standalone image it's the
best-looking; as a background it would fight the text it's supposed to
sit behind, which is the actual job here.
